Pregnancy Diet

Eating well, without the confusion

Pregnancy brings plenty of food advice, and not all of it fits your life. We help you build simple, balanced meals around your preferences, appetite and stage of pregnancy.

Your guidance adapts as you move through each trimester, with attention to common concerns such as nausea, acidity and constipation, and to what your doctor has advised.

“Good nourishment should feel steady and simple, not stressful.”

Please Note

Your safety comes first.

Keep taking the supplements and medicines your doctor has prescribed. If you have gestational diabetes, high blood pressure or anaemia, your doctor’s plan comes first. Please do not start herbal products without medical advice. Seek urgent medical care for vaginal bleeding, leaking fluid, severe headache or blurred vision, chest pain, severe abdominal pain, fainting, regular painful contractions before term, or reduced baby movements.

01

What we collect

When you use our booking form, you may give us your name, age, gender, contact number, email, occupation, marital status, address, the service you are interested in and how you heard about us. During consultations you may share health, lifestyle, diet and pregnancy information.

02

How the booking form works

This website does not store the details you enter in the booking form. When you submit it, WhatsApp opens with your message ready to send, and we receive your details only if you send that message.

WhatsApp is a third-party service with its own terms and privacy policy.

03

Why we use your information

  • To respond to you and schedule sessions.
  • To understand your needs and prepare personal guidance.
  • To provide follow-up support and keep necessary records.
  • To meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.
04

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to provide our services, with your consent, or where the law requires it.

05

How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as needed for your care, our records and legal requirements. [Retention period to be confirmed.]

06

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by writing to previtazen26@gmail.com. Some records may need to be kept where the law requires it.

07

Security

We take reasonable care to protect your information. No online service or messaging platform can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please share only what is needed.

08

Children

Information about a person under 18 should be shared by, or with the consent of, a parent or legal guardian.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will publish the latest version on this page.

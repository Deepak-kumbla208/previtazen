Meditation Classes

A quieter mind, one breath at a time

Meditation does not require an empty mind or hours of free time. We start with short, approachable practices and build a routine that suits your temperament and your schedule.

Classes can focus on breath awareness, body scanning, mantra or guided attention, along with an evening wind-down that supports good sleep habits.

“Stillness is a skill, and it grows with gentle, regular practice.”

What We Focus On

Thoughtful care, one step at a time

Every plan is personal. These are some of the areas we may explore together.

    A steady anchor for attention that you can return to anywhere.

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Practices that help you step back from a busy mind.

  4. 04

Our Approach

More than a yoga class

Yoga therapy adapts traditional practices to the person in front of us. Posture, breath, relaxation and attention are selected according to your needs, rather than following one fixed routine.

Because sessions are mostly one-to-one, we can adjust the pace and the practice as you go, and build something you can realistically continue at home.

“A good practice fits your life, not the other way around.”

Postnatal Yoga

Care for you, after the birth

The months after birth are full of change, and recovery looks different for every mother. Postnatal yoga starts gently and progresses only when your body is ready.

We focus on breath, core and pelvic floor awareness, posture for feeding and carrying, and short rest practices that fit around life with a new baby.

“Recovery is not a race. Small, kind steps count.”

What We Focus On

Thoughtful care, one step at a time

Every plan is personal. These are some of the areas we may explore together.

  1. 01

    Breath and core

    Reconnecting gently with the deep abdominal muscles.

  2. 02

    Pelvic floor care

    Awareness and gradual strengthening.

  3. 03

    Posture support

    Easing the strain of feeding and carrying.

  4. 04

    Restful moments

    Short relaxation that fits around your baby’s day.

Who It May Suit

Guidance that begins with you.

New mothers who have been cleared by their doctor to resume gentle activity, after a vaginal or caesarean birth.

Please Note

Your safety comes first.

Please wait for your doctor’s clearance, especially after a caesarean. Seek urgent care for heavy bleeding, fever, severe pain, chest pain, breathlessness or a painful swollen leg. If you feel persistently low or have thoughts of harming yourself or your baby, contact a doctor or emergency service straight away.

Therapeutic Yoga

Practice that meets you where you are

Therapeutic yoga is not about reaching a perfect posture. Each session is built around what your body needs, using gentle movement, supported postures, breath and rest.

Sessions are mostly one-to-one, so the pace, the sequence and the level of effort can change with you. We begin by understanding your history, your routine and any limits we need to respect.

“The right practice is the one your body can return to, day after day.”

What We Focus On

Thoughtful care, one step at a time

Every plan is personal. These are some of the areas we may explore together.

  1. 01

    Gentle mobility

    Unhurried movement to ease stiffness and build comfort in daily activity.

  2. 02

    Supported postures

    Variations and props chosen so that each posture feels steady and safe.

  3. 03

    Breath awareness

    Simple breathing practices woven through the session.

  4. 04

    Deep rest

    Guided relaxation that gives the body time to settle.

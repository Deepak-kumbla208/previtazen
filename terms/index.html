05

Continuing medical care

Please continue your regular medical care. Do not stop, start or change prescribed medicine without consulting your doctor. Check with your doctor before beginning any new diet, exercise or therapy if you have a medical condition.

If you are pregnant or have recently given birth, you must have clearance from your obstetrician or doctor before starting yoga or physical practice, and you should continue your scheduled check-ups.

In an emergency, contact your doctor, the nearest hospital or local emergency services immediately. Do not rely on WhatsApp, email or this website for urgent care.

If we need to cancel a session, we will offer you a new time.

Follow-ups and Wellness Reviews

Your first consultation includes one follow-up within 7 days, and up to 3 further follow-ups during the 6 months after that consultation. Each follow-up lasts about 20 to 30 minutes and must be scheduled in advance.

Unused follow-ups expire at the end of the 6 months, have no cash value and cannot be transferred. A Wellness Review is a separate paid session, and its fee will be shared when you book.

The information on this website and in our services is supportive and educational. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

02

About our practitioner

Dr. Jyoshna Rai holds a Bachelor of Naturopathy & Yogic Sciences (BNYS) qualification. Consultations do not replace care from your physician, obstetrician, gynaecologist, eye doctor, mental health professional or other treating clinician.

03

Pregnancy and postnatal care

Our pregnancy services are offered alongside regular antenatal and postnatal medical care, never instead of it. Please obtain your doctor’s clearance before starting. References to natural pregnancy and labour describe our supportive approach and are not a promise of any outcome. Medical needs and your doctor’s advice always come first.

04

Medicines and supplements

Do not stop or change prescribed medicine because of anything you read here or discuss with us. Speak to your doctor before starting supplements or herbal products.

05

Yoga and physical practice

Yoga, breathing, gazing and relaxation practices may not suit everyone. Practise within your limits and stop if you feel unwell.

06

Emergencies

This website and our messaging channels are not for emergencies. If you need urgent help, contact your doctor, the nearest hospital or local emergency services immediately.

07

No guaranteed results

Individual experiences differ. Nothing on this website is a guarantee of a particular result.
